ABOUT US

Getting the groundwork done right.

Balaji Earthmover is an earthmoving and construction company specializing in government infrastructure and tender-based projects. The company operates primarily across Uttarakhand and undertakes projects throughout India.

Our work includes excavation, earthwork, land preparation, material movement and site development. Whether it is a small site or a demanding construction project, we focus on doing the job safely, efficiently, and on time — because a machine is only as good as the people operating and managing it.

Our aim is simple: build a company people can trust for their earthmoving and construction needs, and build relationships that last beyond a single project.

  • ✓ Founded in 2008 by Mr. Guga Lal Singhla
  • ✓ Proprietorship, registered in Rudrapur, Uttarakhand
  • ✓ Own fleet — no dependency on rentals
